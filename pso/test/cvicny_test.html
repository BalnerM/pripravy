Cvičný test

9 příkladů napříč sadami 1–8, zadání anglicky jako v testu. Nastav si 100 minut a počítej jen s tahákem, tabulkami a kalkulačkou. Teprve pak se podívej na výsledky a řešení.

Jak pracovat

Nejdřív projdi všechny příklady a začni těmi, které poznáš hned. U každého napiš rozdělení nebo hypotézy, vzorec, dosazení a výsledek. Typ úlohy je v závorce u výsledku a odkazuje na postup v přípravě.

Zadání (MPA-PSO, practice test)

  1. Two numbers \(x, y\) are chosen randomly from the interval \(\langle 0, 3\rangle\). What is the probability that they differ by less than 1, i.e. \(|x - y| < 1\)?

  2. A factory buys components from two suppliers: 60 % from supplier A and 40 % from supplier B. 3 % of components from A and 6 % from B are defective.

    1. What is the probability that a randomly selected component is defective?
    2. A component is defective. What is the probability that it comes from supplier A?

  3. The discrete random variable X has the distribution function \[ F(x) = \begin{cases} 0, & x \in (-\infty, 1\rangle \\ 0{,}1, & x \in (1, 2\rangle \\ 0{,}5, & x \in (2, 3\rangle \\ 0{,}8, & x \in (3, 4\rangle \\ 1, & x \in (4, \infty) \end{cases} \] Find \(p(X = 3)\), \(p(X < 3)\), \(p(X \ge 2)\), EX and DX.

  4. An average of 12 customers come to a shop per hour. Determine the probability that

    1. at least 3 customers come in 15 minutes,
    2. no one comes in 30 minutes,
    3. exactly 12 customers come during the hour.

  5. The random variable X has the density function \(f(x) = a x^2\) for \(0 \le x \le 3\) and \(f(x) = 0\) otherwise. Compute (a) the parameter a, (b) the distribution function, (c) \(p(1 < X < 2)\), (d) EX and DX, (e) the median.

  6. The lifetime of a bulb is a random variable with a normal distribution with mean 1200 h and standard deviation 150 h.

    1. What is the probability that a bulb fails before 1000 h?
    2. What is the probability that the lifetime is between 1000 h and 1400 h?
    3. What warranty period should the producer give so that only 2 % of bulbs fail within the warranty?

  7. A package contains 100 components. The weight of one component is a random variable with mean 2.5 g and standard deviation 0.4 g; the weights are independent. What is the probability that the total weight of the package is more than 255 g? Which total weight is exceeded with probability 0.05?

  8. Nine measurements of a quantity with a normal distribution \(N\!o(\mu, \sigma)\) gave: 8.1; 7.9; 8.4; 8.0; 7.6; 8.3; 8.2; 7.7; 8.1.

    1. Construct a 95 % two-sided confidence interval for μ.
    2. At the significance level α = 0.05, test the hypothesis that μ = 8.3.

  9. According to Mendel's theory, four types of peas occur in the ratio 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. In an experiment, the frequencies 315, 108, 101 and 32 were observed. Use the χ² test to decide at the significance level α = 0.05 whether the data agree with the theory.

Řešení

1 · Rozdíl dvou čísel
Řešení

Ω = čtverec \(\langle 0,3\rangle^2\), plocha 9. Podmínka \(|x - y| < 1\) je pás kolem úhlopříčky; mimo něj jsou dva trojúhelníky s odvěsnou 2:

\[ p = \frac{9 - 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2^2}{9} = \frac{5}{9} \approx 0{,}556 \]
2 · Dva dodavatelé
Řešení

Hypotézy A (0,6), B (0,4), jev V = vadná.

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{(a)}\ p(V) &= 0{,}6 \cdot 0{,}03 + 0{,}4 \cdot 0{,}06 \\ &= 0{,}018 + 0{,}024 = 0{,}042 \\ \text{(b)}\ p(A \mid V) &= \frac{0{,}018}{0{,}042} \approx 0{,}4286 \end{aligned} \]
3 · Distribuční funkce
Řešení

Skoky F: \(p(1) = 0{,}1\), \(p(2) = 0{,}4\), \(p(3) = 0{,}3\), \(p(4) = 0{,}2\).

  • \(p(X = 3) = 0{,}3\)
  • \(p(X < 3) = F(3) = 0{,}5\)
  • \(p(X \ge 2) = 1 - F(2) = 1 - 0{,}1 = 0{,}9\)
\[ \begin{aligned} EX &= 1 \cdot 0{,}1 + 2 \cdot 0{,}4 + 3 \cdot 0{,}3 + 4 \cdot 0{,}2 = 2{,}6 \\ EX^2 &= 0{,}1 + 1{,}6 + 2{,}7 + 3{,}2 = 7{,}6 \\ DX &= 7{,}6 - 2{,}6^2 = 0{,}84 \end{aligned} \]
4 · Zákazníci v obchodě
Řešení

(a) Za 15 minut \(\lambda = 12/4 = 3\); aspoň 3 = doplněk k 0, 1, 2:

\[ 1 - e^{-3}\left(1 + 3 + \tfrac{9}{2}\right) = 1 - 8{,}5e^{-3} \approx 0{,}5768 \]

(b) Za 30 minut \(\lambda = 6\): \(e^{-6} \approx 0{,}00248\).

(c) Za hodinu \(\lambda = 12\):

\[ e^{-12}\frac{12^{12}}{12!} \approx 0{,}1144 \]
5 · Hustota a x²
Řešení

(a) \(\int_0^3 x^2\,dx = 9 \Rightarrow a = 1/9\).

(b) \(F(x) = 0\) pro \(x < 0\); \(F(x) = \int_0^x t^2/9\,dt = x^3/27\) pro \(0 \le x \le 3\); \(F(x) = 1\) pro \(x > 3\).

(c) \(F(2) - F(1) = (8 - 1)/27 = 7/27 \approx 0{,}2593\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{(d)}\ EX &= \int_0^3 \frac{x^3}{9}\,dx = \frac{81}{36} = 2{,}25 \\ EX^2 &= \int_0^3 \frac{x^4}{9}\,dx = \frac{243}{45} = 5{,}4 \\ DX &= 5{,}4 - 2{,}25^2 = 0{,}3375 \end{aligned} \]

(e) \(x^3/27 = 0{,}5 \Rightarrow x = \sqrt[3]{13{,}5} \approx 2{,}381\).

6 · Životnost žárovky
Řešení

X ~ No(1200; 150).

(a) Selže před 1000 h:

\[ p(X < 1000) = \Phi\!\left(\tfrac{1000 - 1200}{150}\right) = \Phi(-1{,}333) = 0{,}0912 \]

(b) Mezi 1000 a 1400 h:

\[ \Phi(1{,}333) - \Phi(-1{,}333) = 0{,}8176 \]

(c) Záruka t, kdy \(p(X < t) = 0{,}02\):

\[ t = 1200 + 150\,u_{0{,}02} = 1200 - 150 \cdot 2{,}054 = 891{,}9\ \text{h} \]
7 · Hmotnost balení
Řešení

Součet 100 hmotností: S ≈ No(100 · 2,5; 0,4 · √100) = No(250; 4).

\[ \begin{aligned} p(S > 255) &= 1 - \Phi\!\left(\tfrac{255 - 250}{4}\right) = 1 - \Phi(1{,}25) = 0{,}1056 \\ s &= 250 + 4\,u_{0{,}95} = 250 + 4 \cdot 1{,}645 = 256{,}6\ \text{g} \end{aligned} \]
8 · Interval a test z dat
Řešení

Kalkulačka (1-Variable): \(n = 9\), \(\bar{x} = 8{,}0333\), \(s = 0{,}2646\) (\(s^2 = 0{,}07\)).

(a) \(t_{0{,}975}(8) = 2{,}306\):

\[ \begin{aligned} 8{,}0333 \pm 2{,}306 \cdot \frac{0{,}2646}{3} &= 8{,}0333 \pm 0{,}2034 \\ \mu &\in (7{,}83;\ 8{,}24) \end{aligned} \]

(b) K1: \(H_0: \mu = 8{,}3\), \(H_1: \mu \ne 8{,}3\). K2:

\[ T = \frac{8{,}0333 - 8{,}3}{0{,}2646}\sqrt{9} = -3{,}024 \]

K3: t(8). K4: \(t_{0{,}975}(8) = 2{,}306\). K5: \(|T| = 3{,}02 \ge 2{,}306\) → \(H_0\) zamítáme. Souhlasí to s (a): 8,3 v intervalu neleží.

9 · Mendelův poměr
Řešení

K1: \(H_0\): pravděpodobnosti jsou 9/16, 3/16, 3/16, 1/16. n = 556, \(E_j = 556\,p_j\) = 312,75; 104,25; 104,25; 34,75.

\[ \begin{aligned} \chi^2 &= \frac{2{,}25^2}{312{,}75} + \frac{3{,}75^2}{104{,}25} + \frac{3{,}25^2}{104{,}25} + \frac{2{,}75^2}{34{,}75} \\ &= 0{,}016 + 0{,}135 + 0{,}101 + 0{,}218 = 0{,}470 \end{aligned} \]

Parametry jsme neodhadovali (r = 0) → \(4 - 1 = 3\) stupně volnosti, \(\chi^2_{0{,}95}(3) = 7{,}815\). K5: \(0{,}470 < 7{,}815\) → \(H_0\) nezamítáme, data s teorií souhlasí.
