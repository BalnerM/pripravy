Formulas

Regression
\[ \begin{aligned} &\text{OLS:} \\ \mathbf w &= (X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top\mathbf y \\ &\text{ridge:} \\ \mathbf w &= (X^\top X + \lambda I_d)^{-1}X^\top\mathbf y \\ &\text{dual:} \\ \boldsymbol\alpha &= (XX^\top + \lambda I_n)^{-1}\mathbf y \\ \mathbf w &= X^\top\boldsymbol\alpha \\ &\text{kernel ridge:} \\ \boldsymbol\alpha &= (K + \lambda I)^{-1}\mathbf y \\ \hat{\mathbf y} &= K_{\text{new}}\boldsymbol\alpha \end{aligned} \] \[ \begin{aligned} &\text{2×2 inverse:} \\ \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}^{-1} &= \frac{1}{ad - bc}\begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} \]
Xrows = samples, first column of ones (intercept)
\(I_d\)identity with the size of the number of weights = np.eye(X.shape[1])
\(I_n\)in the dual, the size of the number of samples = np.eye(X.shape[0])
λpenalty strength: ↑λ = smaller weights, more bias, less overfitting
Classification and metrics (rows = actual)
\[ \begin{aligned} \text{accuracy} &= \frac{TP + TN}{N} \\ \text{recall} &= \frac{TP}{TP + FN} \\ \text{precision} &= \frac{TP}{TP + FP} \\ \text{specificity} &= \frac{TN}{TN + FP} \\ F_1 &= \frac{2TP}{2TP + FP + FN} \\ \text{MSE} &= \frac1n\sum(\hat y_i - y_i)^2 \\ \text{MAE} &= \frac1n\sum|\hat y_i - y_i| \end{aligned} \]
FNa missed positive (“missed”)
FPa false alarm (“incorrectly flagged”)
Preprocessing, descent, filters, trees
\[ \begin{aligned} z &= \frac{x - \mu_{\text{train}}}{\sigma_{\text{train}}} \\ x' &= \frac{x - x_{\min}}{x_{\max} - x_{\min}} \\ w_{t+1} &= w_t - \eta\,\nabla f(w_t) \\ H(X) &= -\sum p\log_2 p \\ I(X; Y) &= H(X) + H(Y) - H(X, Y) \\ G &= \sum_y p(y)(1 - p(y)) \\ G_{\text{2 classes}} &= 2p(1 - p) \\ P(\text{OOB}) &= (1 - 1/n)^n \\ &\approx e^{-1} \approx 0.37 \end{aligned} \]
μ, σ, (x_{min}), (x_{max})always from the training data
ηlearning rate; for \((w - a)^2\) it converges for \(0 < \eta < 1\)
P(OOB)probability that a row is not in the bootstrap sample (out-of-bag)
G0 pure node, 0.5 half and half, 4 : 1 → 0.32

Traps in the samples

Watch out for
  • fit_transform on validation → transform.
  • np.eye(X.shape[0]) in primal ridge → shape[1].
  • argmax = position in remaining, feature = remaining[pos].
  • “Strictly improves”: equality = stop.
  • Degree, λ, k, features – by validation; test once.
  • Orientation of the confusion matrix; always write the denominators.
  • Column order of X as in the task ([1, x] vs. [x, 1]).
  • Random number generator once before the loop; the same idx for X and y.

Useful sentences

TopicSentence
column of onesThe column of ones multiplies the intercept b, so b is learned as an ordinary weight.
linear in weightsThe prediction is a weighted sum of fixed features 1, t, t²; only the features are nonlinear in t.
ridge JThe first term measures the training fit, the second penalizes large weights; larger λ → smaller weights, more bias, less variance.
over/underfitUnderfitting: high training and test error. Overfitting: low training error, much higher test error.
selectionChoose it on a validation split (or cross-validation); use the test set once at the end.
leakageThe mean and standard deviation must be fitted on the training data only and applied with transform.
recallRecall for class 1 is the fraction of truly positive (faulty) items the model detects.
bootstrapSampling with replacement: a row can repeat, and some rows are left out (out-of-bag).
skillA saved recipe (SKILL.md) that the agent loads only when the task needs it.
subagentA helper with its own fresh context that does a side task and returns a short summary.
